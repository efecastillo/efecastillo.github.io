I am a Profesor Asistente at the Departamento de Matemáticas de la Universidad Católica de Chile. I received my PhD in June 2017 from University of California, Davis under the guidance of Fu Liu. Then I spent the Fall of 2017 at MSRI as a postdoctoral fellow. From 2018 to summer 2020 I was a Postdoc in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Kansas. From fall 2020 to summer 2021 I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Nonlinear Algebra group at MPI Leipzig. Here is my CV.
